> This is not the school's official design guide. No right to the DSM logo or brand is granted, and no non-public original document or email is redistributed here.

## Scope
Determine a **visual identity layer** to complement [DSM-ES editorial grammar](../standard/DSM-ES-v0.1.md), and keep source evidence separate from design recommendations.

## Evidence classes
- `PUBLIC_VERIFIED`: identifiable official website, public pages or rights-cleared publications.
- `ARCHIVAL_OBSERVATION`: a pattern independently observed in materials provided for review, without distributing source copies.
- `PROPOSED`: design recommendation not necessarily used by DSM.
- `PENDING_VERIFICATION`: needs institutional confirmation (brand guide, logo master, permitted uses, exact typeface/colors).

## Archival observations — no source files attached
- Historical administrative PDF samples from 2015 and 2018 show a recognizable DSM wordmark with short multicolored bands, bilingual subtitle and spacious white ground. The 2018 regulatory document uses serif body typography, whereas a 2015 guidance document has sans-serif body typography. Thus typography has NOT been demonstrated uniform across document classes.
- A 2025/26 administrative one-page notice uses a monochrome DSM mark, demonstrating the need for good black-and-white output.
- A 2026 presentation is a different format and cannot be treated as a letterhead template.
- 2026/27 newsletters observed in the private archive employ complete German blocks followed by complete Italian blocks with dated headings and signatures. This is an editorial pattern, **not verified as a mandatory DSM template**.
- A September 2026 school communication describes increasing use of WebUntis for parent-school communication: future design must work in digital constrained channels.

These observations are intentionally non-identifying summaries. Personal/teacher correspondence and private file URLs are not part of this public repository.

## Findings
**Confirmed:** school name, bilingual identity, contact point, recognizable logo imagery, varied documentary uses.  
**Not confirmed:** a current official Corporate Identity / Corporate Design manual, exact corporate hex values, approved fonts, required logo clearance/exclusion zone, use rights for the logo, a mandatory stationery template, official PDF/UA conformance.

**Conclusion:** design a **neutral, interoperable editorial visual proposal** that can be matched to the school's approved assets later. Never claim the proposed appearance reproduces or supersedes an official DSM identity.

## Proposed profile rules
| Profile | Language structure | Design intent |
|---|---|---|
| LETTER / BRIEF | DE block, IT block or selected language | Minimal contact header, date, subject and optional signature |
| PARENT NOTICE | Short paired DE/IT blocks or full sequential translations | Compact action, event and deadline visibility |
| WORKSHEET | Optional side-by-side DE/IT on A4 | Shared full-width answer region, appropriate spacing |
| ASSESSMENT | Source-language first; authorized support language only | Consistent score, instructions, preserved competence |
| WEB / WEBUNTIS | Stacked semantic blocks with accessible links | Reflow, no dependency on decorative background or external fonts |

## Proposal: compact A4 stationery
- Use **approved** wordmark if permission is available; otherwise visible text-only neutral placeholder.
- Public contact example (confirm on release): **Deutsche Schule Mailand / Scuola Germanica di Milano**, Via Legnano 24, 20121 Milano, +39 02 6597 614, info@dsmailand.it, https://www.dsmailand.it/.
- Contact channel in a compact 1–2 line header; do not require phone/address in every exercise.
- Subject/date/reference fields in header; body full width where possible; no oversized top band or decorative color fills.
- Footer limited to page count or edition indicator when necessary; support monochrome printing.
- `LRS Adapter` authority controls remain independent of decorative typography.
